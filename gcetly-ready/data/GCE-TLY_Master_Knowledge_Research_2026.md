## B. Institution identity and current snapshot
Government College of Engineering, Tirunelveli (GCE-TLY) is a Government College of Engineering in Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu. The official site describes it as an autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University, Chennai, established in 1981, with a 23-hectare campus. It states that the college is approved by AICTE and DOTE and currently presents seven undergraduate and five postgraduate programmes, with four highlighted NBA-accredited undergraduate programmes: Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Electrical and Electronics Engineering.

Current principal shown by the official website: Dr. P. Latha, M.E., Ph.D., January 2026–present.

Official current homepage snapshot also presents approximately 2500+ students, 150+ faculty members and an 80% placement-rate headline. These headline figures should be stored with the page date because institutional statistics change.

## U. Final ingestion rule
DO NOT treat this document as a single prompt or stuff the whole file into the LLM context.

1. Search the local corpus.
2. Check freshness metadata.
